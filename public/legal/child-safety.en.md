Effective Date: October 10, 2026

Flaxia Child Safety Standards apply to Flaxia (flaxia.app and its official applications), operated by TeamFlaxia. They cover posts, comments, profiles, media uploads, attachments, games, and other interactive content.

## 1. Zero-tolerance policy

Flaxia prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). Such content and conduct are never permitted, including when labeled NSFW or presented as fictional, edited, or AI-generated content.

## 2. Prohibited activity

- Creating, uploading, distributing, requesting, or linking to CSAM
- Grooming, coercing, threatening, or trafficking minors for exploitation
- Using Flaxia to arrange, encourage, or facilitate CSAE
- Using posts, attachments, or interactive content to direct users to prohibited material

## 3. Protection of minors

Flaxia is intended for users aged 13 and older. People under 13 may not register or use the service. These standards protect all users under 18.

## 4. Reporting safety concerns

To report a suspected violation involving a post, open the post's report menu and choose the CSAM reporting category. For other concerns or if in-app reporting is unavailable, email [teamflaxia@proton.me](mailto:teamflaxia@proton.me).

When reporting, provide a URL, username, and description if available. Do not download, copy, forward, or attach suspected CSAM. If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

## 5. Review and enforcement

Flaxia reviews reports of suspected CSAE or CSAM and may restrict or remove content and suspend or restrict accounts. Reports categorized as CSAM are flagged for high-priority moderator attention. Confirmed violations are addressed promptly and in accordance with applicable law.

## 6. Reporting to authorities

Flaxia complies with applicable child protection laws. Where legally required, confirmed CSAM is reported to competent authorities or designated reporting organizations, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) when applicable. Flaxia cooperates with lawful investigations.
